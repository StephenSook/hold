                                                                                             Rate Sheet
As set forth in Section 4 of the Low Budget Theatrical Agreement (“Agreement”), the minimum rates for
Performers (excluding Stunt Coordinators) employed in the Project shall be equal to 65% of the applicable rate
from the Basic Agreement current at the time of performance. Stunt Coordinator rates for Daily, Weekly and “Flat
Deal” Stunt Coordinators track the rates in Schedule K-I, K-II or K-III of the Basic Agreement that are current at
the time of photography. The minimum rates for Background Actors shall be equal to the rates set forth in
Schedule X-I and X-II of the Basic Agreement.Current rates for Schedules X-I, X-II, K-I, K-II and K-III from the
Basic are reflected in the table below.
In the event of a conflict between the below rate sheet and the Agreement, the Agreement shall govern.
